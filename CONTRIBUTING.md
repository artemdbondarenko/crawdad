# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Crawdad.

## Pull requests

Crawdad is a personal project, and **external pull requests are not accepted**. They will be closed
without review, whatever their quality. Please don't spend time on them.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. The project is [MIT-licensed](LICENSE): you are free to use, copy, modify and
build on the code, as long as you keep the copyright and license notice.

## Issues

You may open GitHub issues for bug reports and ideas. They may be looked at from time to time, but
there is no promise of a response or a fix.
